In short

site-que-vende is a kit that measures five patterns in a browser that distinguish a site that brings in customers from a site that looks “pretty and cheap” and was made with AI, then tells you what to fix. It’s for people who own a small business or build sites for small businesses. The kit opens your page on desktop and phone, measures text, color, layers, motion, and free sample, then returns a report with a list of fixes ready to paste into your coding agent (the AI program that edits files for you). To use it, you need Node.js and a terminal.

What it is

An auditor that measures instead of giving opinions

A trained eye gets tired and disagrees. The auditor opens the actual page at 1440×900 and 390×844, then measures it the same way every time.

📏 Measures the rendered page

First screen text, where the button's accent color appears, layers, what moves, and whether visitors have something to try. The score ranges from 0 de 5 to 5 de 5.

🧾 Provides a fix list

Along with a report you can open in a browser, it saves consertar.md: what failed and what to do, ready to paste into Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor.

🧮 Includes a ready-to-use free sample

One file, amostra.js, with a calculator and quiz configured with JSON. No dependencies, no network, no data collection.

The 5 patterns

The five reasons, one by one

For each pattern: the idea, the test number, what the auditor measures, and how to fix it. The numbers come from tests published by the people who ran them. When a number comes from a tool vendor, that is stated.

1

Mirror test

The first screen says who it is for, what they get, and what to do next.

The idea

When the right person lands on the first screen, they need to see themselves: their problem, their world, and a clear sign they are in the right place.

What the tests showed

A job page for truck drivers was rebuilt around the drivers after they were interviewed: half accessed it on a phone, the headline offered no benefit, and the photo was a stock image of a handshake.

12.1% before
21.7% after (79.3% better)
+90%more clicks to the pricing page after changing "Companies grow faster online" to "Create a page for your business"
+31.4%more conversions when the headline repeated the word the person had searched for

What the auditor measures

  • whether there is a headline on the first screen and whether it is not a phrase that could fit any company;
  • whether the headline is 16 words or fewer;
  • whether there is ONE primary button on the first screen, on desktop (1440×900) and phone (390×844);
  • whether the button text says what happens (not "Learn more", "Submit");
  • whether a second prominent button competes with it.

How to fix it

  • Rewrite it using the answers in the brief. Put the result in the headline, not a feature.
  • One button, one action, on the first screen, right below the headline.
  • On a phone, shorten what comes before so the button fits within 390 px.

It does not measure whether the copy is true or speaks to the right person. That is why it returns the three human review questions.

2

Highlighter rule

The accent color appears only on clickable elements.

The idea

If you highlight a line in a book, you can find it in two seconds. If you highlight everything, you have highlighted nothing. AI puts the accent color on the headline, icons, border, background, and button. Then the button disappears.

The numbers

60 / 30 / 1060% background, 30% structure (text, menu, cards), 10% accent, used only on buttons and links
2the maximum number of times the button color can appear on non-clickable elements, according to the auditor
15%of the page covered by the accent color: above that, it fails

What the auditor measures

  • the color of the primary button;
  • how many non-clickable elements use it (headline, text, icon, border, background);
  • whether the button stands out from the background around it;
  • how much of the page is covered by the accent color and how much by the background color.

How to fix it

  • Use only three colors.
  • Remove the button color from headlines, icons, borders, and backgrounds. Use the text color and structure color for everything else.

The image desktop-marca-texto.png marks each incorrect use. See a real example in Before and after.

3

Front, middle and back

Scene in back, content in the middle, button in front.

The idea

On a cheap-looking site, everything is flat on the same plane, and your eye does not know where to start. On a stage, there is the painted backdrop, the furniture, and the actor. On a site: background (photo or shape), middle (the content), front (the button).

The numbers

1at least one element crossing the boundary between two sections
0elements with shadows: everything on the same plane, fail
more than 6shadows, and on more than half the boxes: shadows everywhere, fail

What the auditor measures

  • whether the first screen has a photo, video, shape, or gradient behind the text;
  • how many elements cross the boundary between two sections;
  • how many elements have shadows.

How to fix it

  • Put something real in the background of the first screen: a photo of the work or product, or a soft shape.
  • Have ONE element (card or image) extend halfway into the section below.
  • Use shadows only where they matter: on the button and one card.
4

Motion with a purpose

Text ready instantly, one thing moves per screen, and motion can be turned off.

The idea

Animation is the fastest way for a site to look expensive, and also the fastest way for it to look AI-generated. Every animation needs a job: show the product, point to the next step, or show what just happened.

What the studies showed

  • Motion draws the eye. That is both its advantage and its downside: it pulls attention away from what the person came to do.
  • A university study tested the same site with and without parallax (a background that moves at a different speed as you scroll): people found the parallax version more fun and more professional-looking, but the overall user experience did not improve.
1 in 3adults over 40 in an American survey had some sign of inner ear dysfunction (balance). That is why phones and desktops have a "reduce motion" option.

What the auditor measures

  • whether the headline and button are readable 0.4 s after the page loads;
  • how many things change opacity, position, clipping, or filters on each screen, while scrolling and at rest: fails at 4 or more, warns at 2 or 3;
  • how many keep moving with prefers-reduced-motion: reduce: it must be zero.

How to fix it

  • Show the first screen's headline, text, and button ready to read, without an entrance animation.
  • Let one thing move per screen. Keep everything else still.
  • Inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce), turn everything off.
5

Free sample

Visitors try the result before sharing their contact information.

The idea

Almost every site explains everything and gives you a form. Better: let people try the result before asking them for anything. We value more what we helped build. When someone enters their own numbers, the result belongs to them.

The number

close to 45%conversion on a calculator for the price of an orthodontic appliance on an orthodontist's site, according to the company that made the tool (vendor figure, not an independent study)
7steps or fields: above that, the auditor fails it because people do not reach the end

What the auditor measures

  • whether there is a tool that is not a contact form;
  • whether it changes the result on screen when someone interacts with it (it changes the controls and clicks);
  • its size: how many steps or fields.

How to fix it

  • Find the question every customer asks before buying ("How much does it cost?", "Which one is right for me?").
  • Answer with a quiz, a calculator, or a useful tool. The ready-made samples handle the first two.
  • The calculator's numbers come from the business owner.

User guide · step by step

Use it in 2 minutes

You need Node.js and git installed. These commands are for the bash or zsh terminal (Linux and Mac; on Windows, use WSL).

To check: node -v and git --version must return a version number. WSL is the Linux terminal Microsoft offers within Windows (how to install it). From step 2 onward, run the commands from inside the site-que-vende folder.

1

Download the kit and install the test browser

The auditor uses Playwright to control its own Chromium browser. You only need to install it once.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/site-que-vende && cd site-que-vende
cd skill/site-que-vende/scripts && npm install && npx playwright install chromium && cd -
2

Measure your page

Pass the website address or the path to an HTML file. The report goes in the --saida folder.

node skill/site-que-vende/scripts/auditar.js https://seusite.com.br --saida auditoria

With a file instead of an address:

node skill/site-que-vende/scripts/auditar.js minha-pagina/index.html --saida auditoria

What to know before running

  • Each audit takes 20 to 60 seconds and writes 1 to 12 MB to auditoria/ (screenshots and report). Add auditoria/ to the site's .gitignore.
  • While testing the sample, the auditor blocks every page submission (form, fetch, any request that isn't GET) and every attempt to reach another address. Nothing is sent to the site, and the report lists what was blocked.
  • The auditor scrolls the page and interacts with the sample's controls (sliders, options, buttons) to see if it responds. It does not submit forms or follow links.
  • Which is the main button? It picks the largest filled button on the first screen and says in the report which one it picked. If it picked the wrong one, mark the right one in the HTML with the data-cta attribute (<a data-cta href="...">). On a site you cannot edit, run with --cta "css-selector".
  • A local page that uses files outside its folder (../) and is not in a git repository: pass --raiz <folder that contains everything>. The auditor warns when a file returns 404.
  • Patterns 1 and 4 are measured on desktop (1440×900) and phone (390×844). Patterns 2, 3, and 5 on desktop only.
  • node skill/site-que-vende/scripts/auditar.js --help shows all options.
3

Read the result

The opacity number on the pattern 4 line changes slightly with each run because it depends on when the measurement is taken.

The auditoria/ folder contains: relatorio.html (to open in a browser), relatorio.json (the measurements), consertar.md (the to-do list), and the screenshots desktop-tela1.png, celular-tela1.png, desktop-pagina.png, and desktop-marca-texto.png.

Exit code: 0 means all five passed, 1 means at least one failed, and 2 means a usage error or the page did not open. This works in CI (the automated check that runs whenever the code changes).

4

Fix only what failed

Paste auditoria/consertar.md into your coding agent along with the page. The order follows patterns 1 through 5: text before color, color before layering. Without an agent, open relatorio.html and fix things by hand. Each failure tells you what to do.

5

Measure again until you get 5 out of 5

Repeat steps 2 through 4. Don’t change the auditor or the rules to pass. If a pattern still fails after 3 rounds, stop and check the measurement. It may be your decision.

6

Do a human review before publishing

The auditor doesn’t judge the text. Look at auditoria/desktop-tela1.png and auditoria/celular-tela1.png, then answer the three questions at the end of consertar.md:

  • Who is it for? (Does the right visitor recognize themselves in the headline?)
  • What do I gain? (Is it a result, not a feature?)
  • What should I do now? (One button, one action?)

Using the skill in Claude Code

A skill is a folder with a SKILL.md file: the instructions the agent follows. This kit’s skill tells the agent to create the brief, measure, fix, measure again, then stop for your review.

Install and load

From inside the kit folder, create the shortcut in Claude Code’s skills folder:

mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills
ln -s "$PWD/skill/site-que-vende" ~/.claude/skills/site-que-vende

Claude Code reads ~/.claude/skills/site-que-vende/SKILL.md when a session starts. Open a new session and ask: "audit my site with site-que-vende" or "why isn’t my site converting?". If it doesn’t load automatically, say: "read ~/.claude/skills/site-que-vende/SKILL.md and follow the steps".

Inside SKILL.md, the paths (scripts/, amostras/, referencias/) are relative to the skill folder, which it calls $KIT. The agent works in your site's folder: it calls the auditor with node $KIT/scripts/auditar.js and copies $KIT/amostras/amostra.js into the site.

In another agent, or without an agent

In Codex or Cursor, open the kit folder and say: "read skill/site-que-vende/SKILL.md and follow the steps".

Without an agent, follow the six steps above. The auditor and samples work on their own, and relatorio.html tells you what to do for each failure.

To check the kit: bash tests/rodar.sh runs 38 checks and must finish with TUDO OK.

What never to do to pass. Make up a testimonial, number, client, award, or badge. Put data-cta on a hidden button or hide elements just to pass the auditor. Change the text the site owner wrote when it already answers the three questions. Ask for an email before showing the sample result.

What you can configure in the JSON

TagKeysHow it works
<amostra-calculadora>titulo, subtitulo, base, campos, margem, arredondar, prefixo, sufixo, botao, nota, moeda, idioma, ateresult = (base + sum of fields) × multipliers, shown as a range (plus or minus the margin). Brief

The questions to answer before any design

Answer these before asking AI to build the page, then paste your answers into the prompt. Without them, AI writes for “any business,” and no design can save it.

The three Mirror test questions (required)

  1. Who is it for? One person, not a market. “A hairstylist who works alone,” not “beauty professionals.”
  2. What do they gain? The result in their life, not the feature. “A full schedule without answering messages,” not “AI booking.”
  3. What do they do now? One action, with what happens after the click. “See available times,” not “Learn more”.

The questions that refine the copy and colors

  1. What word does this person use to search for this? Use the same word in the headline.
  2. What question does every customer ask before buying? Turn it into the free sample.
  3. Where will they open the site: phone or desktop? On a phone, does the button fit on the first screen at 390 px?
  4. What question or fear makes them give up? Answer it right below the button, in one line.
  5. What photo shows their world? A photo of your work or product, never a stock photo.
  6. What are the three colors? Background (60%), structure (30%), and accent (10%, buttons and links only).

Limits

Read before you trust it

What the auditor cannot see, in plain terms.

  • It measures form, not truth. A specific but false headline passes. That is why human review exists.
  • Black, white, or gray primary button: pattern 2 becomes a warning because it cannot count where else the color appears.
  • Animation made with <canvas> or WebGL counts as a single thing.
  • Big brand websites often fail several patterns and still sell: they already have the trust that a small business website needs to earn on the first screen.
  • Passing all five does not guarantee sales. It removes five common reasons a site loses customers. The rest comes down to the offer, price, and traffic.
  • Shadow, an element crossing the edge, and one thing moving per screen are design choices in this kit. A well executed flat site can sell. The auditor points out where it differs from the pattern.
  • A site that sells nothing (art portfolio, invitation, visual event page): patterns 1 and 5 do not apply. Run the auditor just to see the measurements.
  • The auditor’s messages are in Portuguese. Audited pages can be in Portuguese, English, or Spanish.
  • The auditor's limits (15% accent color, 2 uses outside clicks, 6 shadows, 7 questions, 4 moving elements) are choices made for this kit, not research-based figures.
  • A local file is served at 127.0.0.1 from the root of the Git repository only while the measurement is running.
